# App icons

These application icons come from Flathub's AppStream catalog:
`https://dl.flathub.org/repo/appstream/x86_64/icons/128x128/<application-id>.png`.
The application IDs, project websites and distribution source are recorded in
`src/app-store.ts`. The icons identify their respective applications and belong
to their projects / contributors; this directory does not imply vendor endorsement
of Axon or of the community's Flatpak packaging. No raster assets were generated.
